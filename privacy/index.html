Privacy Policy

Effective 9 October 2026

In short: LWYL has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your workouts are stored on your iPhone. We (Skywarel) don't receive, see or sell any of your data.

This policy explains how the LWYL app for iPhone ("LWYL", "the app") handles information. LWYL is made by Skywarel ("we", "us").

Data stored on your device

Everything you create in LWYL — workouts, sets, routines, scheduled workouts, body measurements, custom exercises, photos you attach and your settings — is stored only in the app on your iPhone. It is not uploaded to us or to any server we run. It is included in your own iPhone backups (iCloud or computer), which Apple handles under its own privacy terms.

Apple Health

If you allow it, LWYL connects to Apple Health. You choose exactly what to share in the Health permission screen and can change it at any time in the Health app.

  • LWYL reads: body weight, body fat percentage, lean body mass, height, biological sex, steps, workouts recorded by other apps and devices, and for those workouts their route, heart rate, distance and active energy.
  • LWYL writes: the workouts you finish in LWYL, their active energy, and waist measurements you log.
  • How it's used: only to show your progress, steps and imported workouts inside the app, and to save your training to Health.

Health data is never sent to us, never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties.

Location

If you allow location access (only while the app is in use), LWYL uses it to:

  • measure distance and route for outdoor runs and walks you track,
  • note the town of a workout, using Apple's place-name service, and
  • show the weather for a workout, using Apple Weather (WeatherKit).

For the place name and the weather, your approximate position is sent to Apple to look them up. It is not sent to us. LWYL never tracks your location in the background.

Photos

You can attach a photo to a workout using the iOS photo picker; LWYL only gets the photos you pick. If you save a share card, LWYL asks for permission to add that image to your photo library. LWYL cannot read the rest of your library.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, LWYL schedules local notifications on your iPhone for days with a planned workout. They are created on the device; no notification service or server of ours is involved.

Sharing

When you share a workout, routine or share card, you choose where it goes using the iOS share sheet. The shared file or image contains only what's shown in it. We don't receive a copy.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data sold or shared with data brokers.

Information you send us

If you email us at support@getlwyl.app, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and delete it when it's no longer needed.

Deleting your data

You can delete workouts and other items in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. Data LWYL saved to Apple Health stays in Health until you delete it there (Health › Browse › the data type › Delete, or Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices).

Your rights

Because we don't hold your personal data, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. If you have a question or a request about your data, including under the GDPR, contact us and we'll help.

Children

LWYL is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes — for example when a new feature works with another service — we'll update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
