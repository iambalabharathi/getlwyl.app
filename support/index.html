Common questions

Where are my workouts stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside the app. There's no account and nothing is uploaded to us. Your workouts are included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud or computer), so restoring a backup brings them back.

How do I connect Apple Health?

Open Settings › Connected Apps › Apple Health in LWYL, or allow access when the app first asks. To change what LWYL can read or write later, use the Health app › your profile › Apps › LWYL.

Why do runs or walks from other apps show up?

With Auto Import on (Settings › Connected Apps), LWYL shows workouts that other apps and your Apple Watch save to Apple Health, next to your lifts. Choose which workout types to import there, or turn it off.

How do I import routines or a training plan?

On the Routines screen, tap the import button and pick a .lwyl or .json file. You can also open a .lwyl file straight from Messages, Mail, AirDrop or Files. Routines with the same name are added as a copy, like "Push Day (2)", so nothing is overwritten. Plans can include scheduled days and times.

How do scheduled workouts and reminders work?

Schedule a routine from its menu, or "Schedule for Later" from a live workout. You'll get at most one reminder a day: at the time you set in Settings › Reminders, or an hour before when a planned workout has its own time. A missed workout stays for 7 days so you can reschedule it, then it's removed.

Can I switch between kilograms and pounds?

Yes — Settings › Units & Figure. Distances and body measurements follow: km and cm with kg, mi and in with lb.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a workout by long-pressing it in Workouts. Deleting the app removes everything LWYL stored on your iPhone. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.
